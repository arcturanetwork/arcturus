# SAP architecture task-lab rubric

For each brief, produce a one-page design in 20 minutes, then score 0–2 on each dimension:

1. Requirement extraction: measurable constraints and ambiguity are explicit.
2. Correctness: services/patterns satisfy their documented semantics.
3. Tradeoffs: at least one plausible alternative is rejected for a stated reason.
4. Security/governance: identity, data, network, audit and ownership are addressed proportionally.
5. Reliability/operations: failure modes, observability, recovery and verification are concrete.
6. Performance/cost: workload signals, quotas, transfer and commitment risks are considered.
7. Migration/change: rollout, rollback, dependencies and reconciliation are addressed where relevant.

Maximum 14. Passing practice threshold is 12 with no zero in correctness or security. Grade from the `must_address` and `trap` fields only after writing the response. Memorizing these 20 briefs invalidates them as readiness evidence; generate unseen variants for final assessment.
