# NCP-GENL scenario rubric

Score 0–2 for mechanism accuracy, experiment design, quality/safety gate, operational evidence, and explanation of the trap. Pass at 8/10 with no zero for experiment design or quality/safety.

Written performance proves reasoning only. GPU objectives require reproducible environment metadata, commands/configuration, profiler traces, model and dataset identifiers, raw measurements, and cleanup notes. Deployment objectives additionally require load distributions and tail-latency evidence. Keep evaluation data isolated from iterative prompt and tuning work.
