# Google PMLE scenario rubric

Score 0–2 for product/architecture choice, data and lineage treatment, metric design, production reliability, and security/responsible-AI reasoning. Pass at 8/10, with no zero for metric design or security where the scenario raises sensitive-data or safety concerns.

Use the scenarios closed-book and preserve errors by section. Written answers prove decision reasoning only. Google Cloud lab proof requires project/region and service versions, commands or configuration, metadata/artifact links, raw evaluation and monitoring output, cost observations, IAM evidence, and teardown confirmation.
