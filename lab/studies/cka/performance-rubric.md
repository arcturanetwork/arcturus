# CKA performance-lab rubric

For each lab, award 0–2 for context/namespace safety, diagnosis, minimal correct mutation, verification, and speed/document navigation. Maximum 10; require at least 8 with no zero in safety or verification. Record the exact commands and elapsed time only after execution in a disposable cluster.

Static review cannot award a performance score. Repeating memorized tasks also weakens evidence: vary object names, namespaces, fault causes and topology, and complete two unseen two-hour simulations above the program threshold.
