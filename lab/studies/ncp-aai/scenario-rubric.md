# NCP-AAI scenario rubric

Score each lab 0–2 on: architecture choice, safety boundary, failure handling, verification evidence, and explanation of the named trap (10 points total).

A pass requires at least 8/10, with no zero in safety boundary or verification evidence. Conceptual answers are study evidence only. Platform labs require commands, configuration, telemetry, outputs, environment/version metadata, and teardown notes from an NVIDIA-capable environment.

For exam rehearsal, draw scenarios across domains in proportion to the current official blueprint, finish without notes, and preserve misses in the shared error log. Never treat these original scenarios as recalled exam items.
