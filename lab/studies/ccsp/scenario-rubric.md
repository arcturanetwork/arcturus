# CCSP scenario rubric

Score each scenario 0–2 for governance priority, responsibility/authority, lifecycle sequencing, evidence/auditability, and residual-risk explanation. Pass at 8/10, with no zero for governance priority or authority.

Prefer the answer that establishes ownership, scope, process, and verifiable control operation before an attractive point technology. Record misses as knowledge, scope, sequencing, governance, or question-reading errors. Scenario passes demonstrate applied reasoning; they do not establish professional-experience eligibility or replace provider, audit, legal, and live operational evidence.
