# Provider assurance, contract, and exit review

## Assurance evidence checklist

For SOC/SSAE/ISAE or ISO evidence, record system boundary, service/configuration, audit period, opinion, control objectives, exceptions, subservice treatment, complementary user-entity controls, bridge letter and remediation status. Map claims to our controls. Common Criteria/FIPS validation applies to a specified product/module/version and operating mode—not the entire cloud.

## Contract clauses to resolve

- Data ownership, permitted processing/training, controller/processor/custodian roles and subprocessor notice.
- Security baseline, encryption/key control, personnel access, vulnerability/patch expectations and evidence.
- Incident definition, cooperation, evidence preservation, notification clock and regulator/customer support.
- SLA metrics, measurement source, exclusions, remedies, RTO/RPO and recovery-test participation.
- Data locations, transfer restrictions, government/legal request handling and conflicting jurisdiction.
- Right to audit/assurance access, penetration-testing process and remediation transparency.
- Retention, legal hold, export format/throughput/cost, termination assistance, deletion timeline and proof.
- Liability, indemnity, cyber insurance, IP/licensing, escrow/continuity where appropriate, and change control.

## Exit test

Export source data, metadata, identities/permissions, audit evidence and model/config artifacts into documented portable formats; rebuild minimum service elsewhere; reconcile counts/hashes; revoke access; destroy remaining copies subject to holds; obtain evidence. An exit clause without a timed technical rehearsal does not prove reversibility.
