# Cloud forensic custody and communications

Obtain authority and scope first. Coordinate with the CSP because customers may lack physical media, hypervisor and control-plane access. Record source, acquisition method/tool/version, time source/timezone, tenant/account/Region, collector, hashes, transformations, storage, access and every custody transfer. Acquire volatile evidence deliberately, preserve originals read-only where possible, analyze working copies, and respect privacy, employment and jurisdictional limits.

Communications use one accountable owner and an audience matrix: responders receive actionable technical facts; executives receive impact/decisions; provider/vendor receives scoped evidence requests; customers/partners receive confirmed material facts and mitigations; regulators/law enforcement receive legally reviewed content within applicable clocks. Preserve privilege where counsel directs. Never speculate publicly or let a technical team independently decide legal notification.
