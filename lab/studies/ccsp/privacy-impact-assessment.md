# Privacy impact assessment — TrustGraph

## Purpose and necessity

Process approved organizational documents to retrieve evidence-backed answers. Publishing/actions are optional and separately approved. Do not ingest a corpus merely because it is accessible; document lawful purpose, necessity and compatible use.

## Data map

Data subjects may include staff, customers and authors. Inputs may contain identifiers, employment/business content, prompts, feedback, IP addresses and audit identities. Derived data includes embeddings, summaries, evaluation labels and traces. Locations include source store, ingestion staging, index, model context, logs, backups and recovery copies; each processor/subprocessor and Region must be inventoried.

## Controls and rights

- Minimize fields and context; detect/classify before indexing.
- Enforce purpose- and tenant-aware authorization before retrieval and again before actions.
- Separate encryption keys and duties; restrict operator access.
- Apply retention to originals, chunks, embeddings, caches, traces, backups and derived datasets.
- Support access/correction/deletion where applicable while preserving valid legal holds.
- Assess cross-border transfer mechanism, residency promises and conflicting legal demands with counsel.
- Test model-provider data-use settings and contractual training restrictions.
- Provide notices, escalation and meaningful human review for consequential outputs.

Residual risks include memorization, inference, re-identification, inaccurate summaries and jurisdictional access. Privacy/security/legal owners must accept residual risk before launch; this document is a technical exercise, not legal advice.
